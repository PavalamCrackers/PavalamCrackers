Terms & Conditions

Enquiry-Only Platform

As per the Hon'ble Supreme Court's directive (2018), online sale of firecrackers is strictly prohibited. Pavalam Crackers & Traders operates purely as an enquiry-based platform — you may browse products and submit your requirements, but no online sale or payment is processed through this website. Once we receive your enquiry, our team will contact you via WhatsApp or phone call to confirm and process your order offline.

How We Use Your Information

When you use the enquiry cart on this site, we ask for your name, phone number and delivery address so that our team can prepare and confirm your order. This information:

  • Is stored only as a cookie in your own browser — it stays on your device.
  • Is never saved in any database or server operated by us or any third party.
  • Is used only to pre-fill your order list PDF and to help our team reach you on WhatsApp or by phone about your enquiry.
  • Is included in the order PDF you generate and choose to send us on WhatsApp — that copy is then handled the same way as any WhatsApp message you send us.

You can clear this information at any time by clearing your browser's cookies for this site, or by editing the fields directly in the cart before checkout.

Cookies

We use cookies for essential site functionality only — to remember the items in your enquiry cart and the contact details described above. We do not use cookies for advertising or third-party tracking.

Legal Compliance

Pavalam Crackers & Traders follows 100% legal and statutory compliance, and all our shops and go-downs are maintained as per the explosive acts. We send parcels only through registered and legal transport service providers, in line with standard practice among major firecracker traders in Sivakasi.

Questions about these terms? Reach out to us on WhatsApp or phone via the contact details in the footer.
